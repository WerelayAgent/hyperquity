Architecture

The Automated Fee Loop

How every token swap on Solana feeds a high-leverage perpetual on Hyperliquid, and turns winning trades into deflationary buybacks.

01

Launch on Solana

Launch your token on Float/Raydium paired with SOL or USDC. Bind it permanently to any Hyperliquid perp (BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, etc.) at 1x to 20x Long or Short.

Fixed at token genesis
02

Trading Fees Feed Margin

50% of all swap fees on Solana are claimed in real-time and bridged across to Hyperliquid isolated margin. As volume grows, margin grows.

Autonomous API execution
03

+20% Buys Back & Burns

When the perpetual reaches +20% unrealized PnL, the engine takes profit, bridges USDC back to Solana, purchases the native token from the pool, and permanently burns it.

Continuous deflation
